## 🎲 D&D Style Campaign Plugins

### 11. **Star Trek: Starfleet Operations D&D**

_Plugin ID: `star-trek-operations-dnd`_

**Concept**: Traditional D&D mechanics adapted for Star Trek universe operations

- **Universe Integration**: Character classes based on Starfleet divisions (Command, Science, Engineering, Medical, Security)
- **Campaign Types**:
  - **Exploration Campaigns**: Survey missions in user-created sectors
  - **Diplomatic Missions**: First contact with user-created species
  - **Crisis Response**: Emergency situations on user-created colonies
  - **Temporal Investigations**: Time-related anomalies and paradoxes
- **Features**:
  - Starfleet rank progression system (Cadet → Admiral)
  - Species-specific bonuses (Vulcan logic, Klingon strength, etc.)
  - Ship-based encounters with user's fleet
  - Prime Directive moral choice mechanics
  - Away team composition and tactics

**Example Character Classes**:

- **Command Officer**: Leadership bonuses, tactical advantage, crew inspiration
- **Science Officer**: Research abilities, sensor analysis, hypothesis testing
- **Chief Engineer**: System repair, technical solutions, equipment modification
- **Medical Officer**: Healing abilities, xenobiology knowledge, psychological analysis
- **Security Chief**: Combat expertise, threat assessment, protection protocols

4. **Weekend Marathon**: Bridge Command simulator featuring consequences from week's gaming
